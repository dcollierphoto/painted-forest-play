How to play

You are a nature photographer in a painted California. Explore, find animals and beautiful light, and take photos. Good photos earn money for gear and fill your field journal.

Moving: W and S walk, A and D turn, Shift runs, Space jumps, X crouches or lies down. Drag the mouse to look. On a phone, use the stick at bottom right.

Camera: C raises and lowers the camera. Space or the round button takes the photo. Scroll or tap a number to change lens. Tap inside the frame to choose what to focus on, or use Focus for manual (L locks focus, Q and E fine-tune). Tap Macro for tiny things, Tripod for long exposures, Me to put yourself in the shot.

Top buttons: the sun opens settings, the book is your field journal, the map shows where you are, the basket holds berries and mushrooms, the bag is the gear shop. Tap the clock to rest until a better time of day.

Better photos: shoot in the first and last hour of sun, get close, keep the animal clear of grass and trees, and wait for it to do something. Every photo tells you what would improve it.

Earning: open your photos (bottom left) to submit them to the stock agency, post to your blog, and read your assignments.

Your game saves by itself in this browser.

Move my game

On the device that has your game: tap Copy my game code, then send the code to yourself (Notes, Messages or email).
On the new device: paste the code in the box and tap Load this code. Money, gear, journal, map and assignments come across. Photos stay behind.

Send feedback

Thank you for testing. Write what you liked, what confused you and what broke. Then tap Copy and paste it in a message to David.
Tap the map to drop a pin. Tap a pin to remove it.
